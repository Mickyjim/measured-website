A guide for difficult messages

What should I text back?

Start by deciding what the reply needs to do: clarify a misunderstanding, make a plan or pause the exchange. You don't have to answer at once.

Give yourself time before choosing words

If you are angry or unsure, draft a holding message first. Avoid committing to a conversation or a deadline you cannot manage. Then return to the specific question that needs an answer.

A short example

Illustrative wording to adapt to your situation

Message received
“Are you seriously not going to answer me?”
A possible reply
“I've seen your message. I need some time to think before I reply properly.”

This acknowledges the message without promising an instant answer. If a practical deadline matters, give a time you can actually keep.
