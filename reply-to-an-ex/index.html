A guide for difficult messages

How to reply to an ex without reopening the argument

First decide whether a reply is necessary. A question about shared responsibilities may need a practical answer; an invitation to revisit an old argument may not.

Separate logistics from relationship history

If you share parenting or other responsibilities, answer the relevant practical point. You can acknowledge the request without agreeing to discuss the relationship. Avoid making promises you do not want to keep.

A short example

Illustrative wording to adapt to your situation

Message received
“You never cared anyway. What time are you picking up the kids?”
A possible reply
“I'll pick them up at 5 as planned. Let's keep this conversation about the arrangements.”

It answers the practical question and states the scope of the conversation without debating the accusation.
