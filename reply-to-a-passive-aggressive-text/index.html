A guide for difficult messages

How to reply to a passive-aggressive text

Tone is easy to misread over text. Instead of deciding what the sender secretly means, ask a direct question about the issue or respond to the practical request.

Ask for the concern behind the comment

A neutral clarification gives the other person room to be specific. If the conversation turns into repeated personal remarks, you can set a limit or pause it. You do not need to return sarcasm with sarcasm.

A short example

Illustrative wording to adapt to your situation

Message received
“Must be nice to have so much free time.”
A possible reply
“Is there something specific you need from me? I'd rather talk about that directly.”

It invites a concrete request without accepting a criticism or asserting that you know the sender's motives.
