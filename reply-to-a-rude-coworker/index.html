A guide for difficult messages

How to reply to a rude coworker professionally

Keep the reply about the work: what needs changing, when it is needed and what you can commit to. You can ask for specifics without accepting a personal remark.

Bring the conversation back to the task

Avoid defending your entire performance in one message. Ask for a concrete change or clarify competing priorities. If personal remarks recur, consider the appropriate support or reporting route at your workplace.

A short example

Illustrative wording to adapt to your situation

Message received
“This is useless. Do it again by tonight if you're actually competent.”
A possible reply
“Please identify the changes you need. I can confirm a realistic timeline once the scope is clear.”

It moves the exchange toward actionable feedback and avoids agreeing to an unexamined deadline.
