Not a text rewriter. A reply coach.

Most tools just reword your message. Measured reads the dynamic, names the trap, and helps you choose a reply that protects both you and the relationship.

Reads the dynamic

It spots pressure, guilt, blame, urgency, and boundary tests — the pattern behind the words, not just the words.

Mind-reading demand

The pressure is to prove love by guessing correctly.

I care about this, and I also don't want to guess and get it wrong. Tell me the specific thing that hurt you and I'll listen.
Why it works: caring stays visible, but the guessing game stops.

Urgency + loyalty test

The trap is accepting an unreasonable demand to avoid looking difficult.

I can prioritize this tomorrow morning. I can't commit to tonight without dropping existing work. If it's critical, tell me what should move.
Why it works: it gives options without volunteering burnout.

Debt framing

The message tries to make disagreement feel like betrayal.

I'm grateful for what you've done. I'm still allowed to make this decision differently. I'd like us to talk without turning it into a debt.
Why it works: it respects history without surrendering autonomy.

Built for trust

Imported chats are used to help the user understand their own relationship context, not to sell, expose, or publicly train on private conversations.

  • Local-first parsing whenever possible.
  • Clear permission before reading a WhatsApp export or using hosted coaching.
  • User-controlled deletion of feedback and memory.

Private by design.

Measured deals with intimate, emotionally loaded conversations. The product and the website lead with privacy, consent, and control: no hidden sharing, no public training on user chats, and no storage without a clear reason.

Important boundaries.

Measured is a conflict communication coach. It helps people slow down and respond better, but it does not replace emergency, legal, medical, or mental-health support.

Does Measured read WhatsApp directly?

No. Users manually import a supported text export. Measured does not connect to a WhatsApp account.

Are voice notes in WhatsApp exports analyzed?

No. The supported WhatsApp import processes text only.

Is this therapy?

No. Measured gives communication coaching support. It is not therapy, legal advice, medical advice, or emergency support.

When does hosted AI run?

Only when configured, signed in, entitled, and within usage limits. Local analysis remains available as fallback.
